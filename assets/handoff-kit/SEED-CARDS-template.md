# Seed cards — per-Matter reading lists and inputs

Sidecar to `HANDOFF.md`. One card per Matter.

- **Read** = exactly the sections the work needs. Read nothing else for
  that Matter.
- **Settled** = inputs to encode without re-litigating.
- **Open calls** = resolve with a short rationale, or carry as an
  explicit early Step.
- **Notes** = anything about sequencing, fan-out, or traps.

All cards assume HANDOFF §1 (operating constraints) and §2 (locked
decisions) as ambient context, plus toolsmith's CONTRACT.md
(`toolsmith doc CONTRACT.md`).

> GUIDANCE — cards worth writing carefully:
>
> - **The chassis card.** Its Brief becomes the most-read document in the
>   register. Say so on the card, and say "write it as
>   conventions-to-re-read, not narrative".
> - **Any card with a fork in it.** When a Matter exists to close a
>   question (which storage model, which library), the card carries the
>   evaluation rubric and where the decision record lands.
> - **The first card a fresh session will open.** It is the one that
>   discovers whether the reading list actually works.
>
> A good "Read" list is three to six items. When a card needs ten, the
> Matter is too big.
>
> A recommendation on an open call ("recommend: X") is not a decision —
> it saves the receiving session a cold start while leaving the call
> theirs.
